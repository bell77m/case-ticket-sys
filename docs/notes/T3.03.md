# T3.03 Dashboard UI

Date: 2026-09-27 · Requirements: FR-P1, FR-P2, FR-I5, FR-I6 · Agent: fe

## What was done
- Staff with report.view (Root Admin, Admin, Team Lead, Viewer) open `/staff/reports` from the account menu. Agents get the no-permission page.
- A filter row (from/to dates, building, category, kept in the URL) scopes the whole page and calls `GET /api/staff/reports` with the browser's time zone and locale. On a refetch the old view stays at reduced opacity, with no skeleton flash.
- 7 summary cards show each value and its change against the previous period as a glyph, a signed number and text (never color alone). The written summary from T3.02 sits below them.
- 7 Chart.js graphs, per REQUIREMENTS §5:
  - opened vs resolved per day
  - open by status
  - open by priority
  - by category
  - by location, with a building → floor → line drill-down
  - staff workload by priority
  - weekly medians, on one y-axis
  Each shows exact values on hover and has a "Show as table" toggle that swaps the canvas for a real table.
- Colors are `--chart-*` tokens read by one reusable `Chart` component. The palettes passed the dataviz validator: the two-series pair, the status colors, and the ordinal priority ramp. Single-series bars use one color.

## Decisions
- Card links go to the closest queue filter, because the queue shows tickets as they are now and has no date, building or category filter:
  - Open → `/staff?status=open`; Unassigned adds `assignee=none`; Urgent open adds `priority=urgent`.
  - New → `?status=new`; Resolved → `?status=resolved`.
  - The two median cards have no link.
  - This differs from FR-P1 ("click opens filtered queue"); the question is under Blockers in PROGRESS.md. Reverse by adding date, building and category filters to `GET /api/staff/tickets` and the queue page, then linking every card exactly.
- The change figure is not colored (up is not always good or bad): it uses a glyph, a signed number and "vs previous period".
- Palettes, with their validator results:
  - lines: #3b82f6 and #fb923c (pass; contrast WARN relieved by legend and table view);
  - status: the badge dot colors (pass);
  - workload: #60a5fa, #2563eb, #1e40af, #172554 (ordinal pass), plus #9ca3af for not triaged.
  - Change them in tokens.css only.
- Wide tables scroll inside a focusable region; an invalid date range shows an error alert.
- The two layout.spec tests time out at 30 s with 6 workers, also without this change, so they are marked `test.slow()`.
